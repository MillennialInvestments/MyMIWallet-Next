# How It Works pages

Public-facing routes and purpose for each page:

- `/how-it-works` — Overview landing that summarizes the MyMI Wallet workflow and links into the subpages.
- `/how-it-works/budgeting` — Explains budgeting flows, transaction organization, and goal tracking.
- `/how-it-works/investing` — Highlights portfolio tracking, analytics, and research views.
- `/how-it-works/crypto` — Covers crypto wallets, swaps, and safety cues.
- `/how-it-works/alerts` — Walks through trade/market alert setup and monitoring.
- `/how-it-works/projects` — Describes collaborative projects and investment commitments.
- `/how-it-works/security` — Surfaces trust, compliance, and recovery practices.
- `/how-it-works/pricing` — Presents plan tiers, comparisons, and calls to upgrade.

All pages share the public layout wrapper and reuse the shared How It Works nav + partials for hero, CTA, and FAQ sections.
