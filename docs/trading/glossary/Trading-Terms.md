# Trading Terms

## VWAP
Volume-Weighted Average Price; the average price weighted by volume, used as an institutional benchmark.

## Relative Volume
Current volume divided by average volume for the same time period, indicating unusual activity.

## EMA Stack
A sequence of exponential moving averages ordered in trend direction, used to confirm alignment.

## Momentum Expansion
Acceleration in momentum indicators such as MACD histogram and RSI moving away from the midline.

## Institutional Inflow
Sustained buying pressure from large participants, often seen with rising volume and VWAP support.
