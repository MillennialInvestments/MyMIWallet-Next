# MyMI Daily Trading Workflow

## Pre-Market
- Review higher-timeframe trend and macro news catalysts.
- Load MyMI – Momentum & Inflow Core datapoint group.
- Flag watchlist symbols with high relative volume pre-market.

## Market Open
- Wait for VWAP and EMA stack to establish.
- Confirm inflow surge with volume and ADX expansion.
- Execute only when entry qualification rules are met.

## Intraday Management
- Trail stops using ATR and structure levels.
- Scale out at key levels (day high/low, previous close).
- Avoid new entries during VWAP chop.

## Exits
- Exit on VWAP loss or EMA stack flip.
- Reduce exposure if momentum indicators fade.

## Post-Market Review
- Capture screenshots and annotate trades.
- Log deviations from trade rules.
- Update scanner/watchlist performance notes.
