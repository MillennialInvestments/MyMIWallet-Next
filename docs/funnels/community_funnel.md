# Community Funnel Blueprint

1. **Awareness**: Platform-native hooks (Shorts/Reels/Posts) seeded with targeted hashtags and $TICKER where relevant.
2. **Engagement**: Polls, questions, and replies drive comments and followers into platform groups/communities.
3. **Conversion**: Group pinned posts route to Discord invite and free tools.
4. **Activation**: Discord welcome flow links to website onboarding and budget tool.
5. **Monetization**: Email/newsletter + in-app upgrade CTAs for Starter/Basic/Pro/Premium.
6. **Retention**: Weekly recap posts, live events, and member-only alerts.

Every stage logs to Management → Communities Hub for auditing and performance notes.
