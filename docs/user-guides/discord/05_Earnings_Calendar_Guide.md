# Earnings Calendar Guide

Stay on top of earnings with weekly and daily posts delivered to Discord.

## What is posted
- **Weekly summary (Monday mornings):** “This Week’s Earnings to Watch” with symbols grouped by day, company name, and BMO/AMC timing. Watchlist and open-alert symbols are highlighted.
- **Daily post (optional):** Morning “Today’s Earnings” snapshot with the same timing details and quick notes (e.g., high IV, recent alert sentiment).

## Where to find it
- Weekly posts appear in channels like `#earnings-watchlist`.
- Daily posts (when enabled) appear in `#daily-earnings` or the designated earnings channel.

## How it relates to your watchlist
- Earnings picks are prioritized using your watchlists and any open trade alerts so you see what matters most.
- Links point back to MyMI dashboards for deeper research and alert management.

## Tips for users
- Add symbols to your watchlist to influence which earnings are highlighted.
- Pair earnings posts with `/mymi alerts` to see active trades before results.
- Expect concise bullets to keep messages under Discord’s character limit.
