# Marketing News & Daily Stories in Discord

Discover how MyMI delivers market news and marketing content straight to Discord.

## How stories are gathered
- **MarketAux API:** Pulls market and ticker-specific headlines for fast coverage.
- **Email scraper:** Collects broker and newsletter content, cleans it, and ranks it for relevance.
- **Alert-derived stories:** Trade alerts can be converted into marketing copy for social and Discord.

## What you’ll see
- **Today’s Story:** A single, high-signal headline with a short summary and link to dive deeper on the MyMI marketing dashboard.
- **Daily top stories digest:** Morning list of the most relevant headlines with links for more detail.
- **Breaking updates:** High-sentiment or watchlist-matching headlines may appear quickly in the news channel.

## Why it’s useful
- Saves time: summaries are already condensed for quick reading.
- Keeps you in one place: links route back to the MyMI dashboard where you can explore source articles and related research.
- Fits every tier: free users get concise headlines; paid members see richer context and more symbols.

## Example format
```
📰 Today’s Story: {Headline}
{2–3 sentence summary}
Read more: https://www.mymiwallet.com/News
```
