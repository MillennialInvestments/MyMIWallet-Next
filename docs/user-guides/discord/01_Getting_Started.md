# Getting Started: Joining the MyMI Discord

This quick-start guide walks you through joining the server, finding the right channels, and linking your MyMI Wallet account.

## Join the server
1. Accept the MyMI Wallet Discord invite link from the website or email.
2. Open Discord (desktop or mobile) and log in with your Discord account.
3. Select **Join** to enter the server.

## Navigate the server
- **Home & announcements:** Server updates, rules, and release notes.
- **Trade alerts:** Real-time signals in tiered alert channels.
- **Marketing & news:** Daily digests and “Today’s Story” summaries.
- **Earnings watch:** Weekly and daily earnings highlights.
- **Support:** Dedicated space for onboarding and troubleshooting.

> 📌 **Tip:** Use the channel list to star your most-used rooms (e.g., alerts and earnings) so they stay pinned.

## Support-first onboarding
- New members are automatically routed by **MEE6** to the **Support** category landing channel (e.g., `#welcome-support`).
- The landing channel posts server rules, how to link your account, and how to get live help.
- Ask questions in the Support threads or tag a moderator.

## Create your MyMI Wallet account
1. Visit **https://www.mymiwallet.com** and select **Create Account**.
2. Complete the signup, then verify your email.
3. Choose your membership tier (you can upgrade later).

## Link Discord to MyMI Wallet
1. In Discord, run the slash command `/mymi link`.
2. Click the secure link you receive and sign in to MyMI Wallet.
3. Approve the connection. A success message confirms the link.

### Troubleshooting linking
- **Didn’t get the link?** Check that you ran `/mymi link` in a channel where the bot is allowed to reply.
- **Link expired?** Run `/mymi link` again to generate a new token.
- **Signed into the wrong MyMI account?** Log out in the browser, sign back in with the right account, and re-run the command.
- **Still stuck?** Post in `#welcome-support` with a screenshot placeholder below.
