# MyMI Wallet (CI4) Overview

The MyMI Wallet web application powers budgeting, investments, alerts, marketing content, and earnings tracking. It also links directly with Discord so you can access the same data through slash commands.

## How the CI4 app connects to Discord
- Discord commands call CI4 APIs for alerts, portfolios, watchlists, earnings, and news.
- Webhooks push automated alerts, news, and earnings posts into Discord channels.
- Account linking keeps your Discord identity mapped to your MyMI account so replies can include your data.

## What you can do in the app
- **Alerts:** Review, filter, and create trade alerts that also notify Discord.
- **Marketing & news:** Generate and edit marketing stories and news digests that publish to Discord and other channels.
- **Earnings:** Track upcoming earnings with watchlist-aware highlights that mirror Discord posts.
- **Investments & budgeting:** Log trades, manage portfolios, and monitor performance alongside Discord summaries.

## User dashboard highlights
- Recent alerts, portfolio snapshots, news summaries, and earnings tiles.
- Quick links to connect Discord and manage notification preferences.
- Indicators showing whether Discord automations are healthy.
