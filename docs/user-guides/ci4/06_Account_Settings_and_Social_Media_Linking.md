# Account Settings & Social Media Linking

Control your profile, membership, and connected accounts.

## Profile settings
- Update your display name, contact email, and notification preferences.
- Review security options and enable email alerts for account changes.

## Membership tiers
- View your current plan and renewal date.
- Upgrade to unlock premium alert details, faster updates, and richer Discord content.

## Link Discord
1. Open **Account → Social Media** inside MyMI Wallet.
2. Click **Link Discord** or run `/mymi link` in Discord to generate a secure token.
3. Confirm in the browser to bind your Discord user to your MyMI account.
4. Linked status appears on the Social Media page so you can verify or disconnect.

## Manage connected accounts
- Review all linked platforms (Discord, social channels) and revoke access if needed.
- Keep logins current to avoid failed notifications.
