# Earnings Dashboard Guide

Track upcoming earnings and keep Discord in sync.

## Read the calendar
- The dashboard lists upcoming earnings with date and BMO/AMC timing.
- Symbols on your watchlist or with active alerts are highlighted.

## How it ties to Discord
- Weekly “This Week’s Earnings” and optional daily posts pull from the same calendar data.
- Updates you make to watchlists influence which tickers appear in Discord posts.

## Customize your watchlist focus
- Add or remove symbols to refine what’s emphasized in weekly/daily summaries.
- Use filters to focus on sectors or tags before generating Discord posts.

## Quick actions
- Open a symbol to review recent alerts or news before earnings.
- Export or share links to the earnings view for team collaboration.
