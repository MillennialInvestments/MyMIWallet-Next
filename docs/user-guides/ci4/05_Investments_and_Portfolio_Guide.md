# Investments & Portfolio Guide

Manage your trades and portfolios inside MyMI Wallet and mirror key data to Discord.

## Add trades
- Use **Add Trade** to log entries, sizes, and notes.
- Attach stop losses and targets so alerts stay consistent across web and Discord.

## Monitor performance
- View portfolio value, P&L, and top holdings at a glance.
- Drill into individual positions to see history and related alerts.

## Manage watchlists
- Create or update watchlists to influence alert and earnings prioritization.
- Watchlists sync with Discord commands like `/mymi watchlist` and ticker lookups.

## How Discord commands expose this data
- `/mymi portfolio` shows your summary or positions after account linking.
- `/mymi watchlist` lists tracked symbols and recent alert/news context.
- `$TICKER` messages surface recent alerts or charts tied to your watchlist symbols first.
