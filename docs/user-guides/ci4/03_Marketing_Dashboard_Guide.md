# Marketing Dashboard Guide

Use the Marketing dashboard to build and distribute news and marketing content to Discord and other channels.

## Generate daily content
- Select **Generate Today’s Content** to pull fresh stories from MarketAux and internal scrapers.
- Review the suggested headlines and summaries before publishing.

## Edit and approve summaries
- Open any story to tweak the headline, summary, or links.
- Keep Discord in mind: concise, under 2000 characters, and readable.

## Trigger distribution
- Choose platforms (Discord, social, Zapier/email) for each piece of content.
- For Discord, pick the target channel (e.g., marketing/news) and send.
- Use staging webhooks when testing new templates.

## Manage posts
- Track what was sent, where, and when inside the dashboard.
- Re-send or schedule digests if a channel missed the original post.

## Connect to social and Discord
- Link your Discord account to unlock quick sends from the dashboard.
- Map social accounts if you want parallel publishing to multiple platforms.
