# Where to Share What? (Content Governance)

Use this guide to direct posts to the right channels and keep the server organized.

## Alerts & trade ideas
- Use `#trade-alerts-free` for community-level alerts and free signals.
- Paid tiers belong in `#trade-alerts-tier1/2/3` (match the member’s plan).
- Quick intra-day updates can land in `#alert-lifecycle` if they track status changes.
- Always include `$TICKER` or `/mymi alerts` context.

## Due diligence & research
- Post long-form DD, thesis, and links in `#due-diligence` (or `#research` if separate).
- Summaries are welcome in alert channels; link back to the full DD thread.

## General chat & questions
- Direct new members to `#welcome-support` first.
- Use `/mymi support` to open a request or tag @Support for quick help.
- Keep off-topic chatter outside automation-heavy channels.

## Streaming announcements
- Share Twitch/YouTube go-live links and replays in `#live-streams`.
- Follow pinned stream rules and cross-link to alerts/DD channels when referenced on-air.

## Off-topic or non-financial posts
- Keep memes/off-topic material out of alert, research, and support channels.
- If needed, create a dedicated `#off-topic` channel with light moderation.
