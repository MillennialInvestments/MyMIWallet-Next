# LinkedIn Platform Format Rules

- **Max characters**: Keep under 3,000; lead with a 2–3 line hook.
- **Hashtags**: 3–5 targeted; avoid keyword stuffing.
- **Links**: 1–2 with UTM; place near end to avoid burying hook.
- **Mentions**: Up to 5 companies/people for reach; ensure relevance.
- **Tickers**: $TICKER or plain text; avoid more than 3 per post.
- **Content mix**: Carousels, short text posts, document uploads, occasional video.
- **CTA block**: Custom button to Discord/Website; include Free Tool + Membership in body.
