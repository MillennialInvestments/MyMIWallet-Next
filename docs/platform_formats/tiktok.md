# TikTok Platform Format Rules

- **Max characters**: Caption up to ~2,200; keep hook under 150.
- **Hashtags**: 3–6 niche tags; avoid broad spam.
- **Links**: No inline links; call out "link in bio" and keep bio updated.
- **Mentions**: @user allowed; keep under 3.
- **Tickers**: Plain text tickers (AAPL) in caption or on-screen text.
- **Clips**: 30–60s, captions burned in; first 1–2 seconds carry the hook.
- **CTA block**: Use comment pin to route to Discord + website + free tool.
